AI Agents currently covers crypto and Web3 agents, agent frameworks, and agent
marketplaces with a documented onchain use case. General-purpose AI assistants
and frameworks are outside this category for now. Agent wallets, payment rails,
and data infrastructure retain their existing lead categories unless agents
are their primary product.
