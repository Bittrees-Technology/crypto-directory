## How to review

1. Confirm that the linked site belongs to the project and is not an existing listing under another name.
2. Open the actual product or documentation and check activity, category fit and exclusion criteria. The homepage check alone is insufficient.
3. Correct the proposed description and unknown metadata. Founding year, country, token and social accounts have not been guessed.
4. Record acceptance or rejection and evidence through the existing review process. For the repository intake path, accepted individual entry objects go to `content/submissions/approved/` and are imported as `pending_audit`. Do not move this batch there or run automatic first-pass verification on it.

Merging the research/category change does not approve these 500 candidates. Research files are not copied into public directory data.

## Evidence limits and attribution

Each selected primary site returned HTTPS 200 and an HTML title during this run. Redirects and duplicate domains were screened. No sign-in, wallet connection, product execution, paid API call or safety audit was performed. A site can change after collection. Some sites require JavaScript; product/docs links remain unverified until review.
