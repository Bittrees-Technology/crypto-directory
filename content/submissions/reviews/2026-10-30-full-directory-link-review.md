Public discovery contains 1272 verified listings. 198 records remain in Needs update, including 13 prior holds. 61 records are rejected, including 30 prior rejections.

## Review method and limits

Checked 2,616 unique listed website/product URLs, followed redirects and read current content. Followed up on 459 original URLs in an isolated browser, 141 permanent redirects and selected official replacement routes. The newer cohort was also compared with its recorded 29 October editorial decisions.

A reachable page is not enough: retired services, withdrawal-only products, unrelated-domain reuse, missing product routes and unresolved identity changes were separated. Current official pages retrieved through the web service supplied additional evidence where direct requests were blocked. Neither a bot/region gate nor a network error is called a shutdown. Unresolved listings are held for follow-up; only verified entries appear in public discovery.

This is an editorial availability review, not a contract/security audit or financial endorsement. No accounts, wallet connections, signatures, purchases or transactions were used. A working interface does not establish the safety or performance of its products.

The JSON records each stable source ID, cohort, before/after status and hashes, precise changes, reason, and observed source URLs/statuses/dates. A changed redirect target observed through another request is identified as such. Prior source documents, intake packages and editorial reviews are preserved.
